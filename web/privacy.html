Privacy at ECO · Proof of Planet

ECO · Proof of Planet is a hackathon build by Eco Technologies (Nairobi, Kenya). It lets people earn ECOIN for verified climate action, trade recyclable and organic waste with businesses, and pay in shops, through WhatsApp and this website.

What we collect

  • Your WhatsApp number, to reply to you and to identify your wallet. It is never published.
  • Messages, photos and locations you send, to check climate actions and waste lots.
  • Ledger entries for ECOIN you earn, trade or spend.

How it is used

  • Photos and the place you share become a public Evidence File so anyone can check the work. You agree to this before anything is checked.
  • Public pages show a pseudonym (like eco_3f9a…), never your phone number. Pickup points are rounded to about 1 km.
  • Photos and messages are analysed by Meta AI models (Muse Spark, SAM 3.1, Muse Image) through the Meta Model API. This build runs on Meta's free developer credits; under those terms Meta may use what is sent to its models to improve its products.
  • We do not use face recognition, do not store biometric data, and do not sell personal data.

Your choices

You can stop at any time by not replying. To ask for your data or its deletion (except ledger entries, which are append-only by design and carry only your pseudonym), open a request on our GitHub repository or message ECO on WhatsApp with the word privacy.

ECOIN in this build is a test ledger. Every ECOIN is minted by a verified climate action.
